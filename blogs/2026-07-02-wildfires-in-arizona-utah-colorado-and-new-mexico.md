Hot, dry, and windy conditions fueled wildfires across Arizona, Utah, Colorado, and New Mexico in late June and early July. NASA's FIRMS fire-detection data, drawn from Suomi NPP thermal imagery, helped track fires including Utah's nearly 80,000-acre Babylon Fire and Colorado's roughly 48,000-acre Aspen Acres Fire.
